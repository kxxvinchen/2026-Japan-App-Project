Hi chaperones,

This is the app the directors will use to communicate with everyone on the tour. It also has the full itinerary, daily attire, hotels, flights, an emergency/procedures page, and more. Below is a step-by-step guide to download it **and** to sign in to your private chaperone channel.

**Link:** https://japan-tour-2026.web.app

---

### 📱 iPhone (use Safari)

1. Open **Safari** and go to **https://japan-tour-2026.web.app**
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow ↑, at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** (top right). An app icon appears on your Home Screen.
5. Open the app from that **icon** (not from Safari).

### 🤖 Android (use Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome** and go to **https://japan-tour-2026.web.app**
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Tap **Install / Add**. An app icon appears on your Home Screen.
5. Open the app from that **icon**.

---

### 👥 Sign in as a chaperone

Signing in gives you access to your **private chaperone channel** (which students can't see) **plus** your ensemble's regular announcements. The app automatically figures out which group you are based on the email you use — there's nothing else to set.

1. Open the app from your **Home Screen icon**.
2. Go to the **Group** tab (bottom right).
3. Under **Directors & chaperones**, tap **Sign in**.
4. Enter the email and password for your group (below), then tap **Sign in**.

**Your logins** (use the one for your group):

| Group | Email | Password |
|---|---|---|
| Band | bandchaperones@gmail.com | chaperonelogin |
| Orchestra | orchestrachaperones@gmail.com | chaperonelogin |
| Choir | choirchaperones@gmail.com | chaperonelogin |

Band and Orchestra chaperones share the **SWE/SSO Chaperones** channel; Choir chaperones get the **Choir Chaperones** channel. After signing in, open the **Announcements** tab and your channel will be there.

---

### 🔔 Turn on notifications (so you get director announcements)

1. Open the app from your **Home Screen icon**.
2. Go to the **Group** tab.
3. Tap **Enable notifications** → **Allow**.

That's it — you're set! Reach out if you have any trouble installing or signing in.
